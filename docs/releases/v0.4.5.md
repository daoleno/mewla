# Mewla 0.4.5

Mewla opens without error sheets, long Brain chats open quickly, a message sent while Brain works shows once, and a dot tells you when an update waits; update Mewla on your computer too.

## New

- **A dot tells you an update waits.** When a new version of the app or of Mewla on your computer is ready, a small dot sits on the cat, and the same dot on the Settings row until you install it. The update is at the top of Settings.

## Fixed

- **Opening no longer shows "Mewla hit an error."** When the app recovers from a problem on its own, it quietly reports it to your computer instead of covering the screen.
- **Long Brain chats open without stalling.** Opening a long chat does a fraction of the work it did, and older messages load as you scroll back.
- **A message sent while Brain is busy shows once.** It could appear twice in the chat. Update Mewla on your computer for this one.
- **Switching computers is instant.** The app switches the moment you choose another computer.
- **Text on Android 15 stays on one line.** Short labels and Git diff lines no longer wrap onto a second line after you type.
- **Tapping a Calendar run opens it.** A tap right after opening an item could do nothing.

## Get it

- **Already installed?** Run `mewla update` on your computer, and install the new APK on Android.
- **New here?** `curl -fsSL https://mewla.ai/install.sh | sh` picks the right file for you. See [Install](https://mewla.ai/docs/install/).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`.
- **Linux x64:** `mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz`
- **Linux ARM:** `mewla-linux-arm64.tar.gz`
- **Windows x64:** `mewla-windows-amd64.zip`, or in PowerShell: `irm https://mewla.ai/install.ps1 | iex`
- **Android:** `mewla-android-arm64-v0.4.5.apk`. Android asks you to allow installs from unknown sources.

All changes: [v0.4.4...v0.4.5](https://github.com/daoleno/mewla/compare/v0.4.4...v0.4.5)

<summary>Verify downloads</summary>

`mewla update` and the installer check downloads for you. To check one by hand against `SHA256SUMS`:

```sh
grep 'mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz$' SHA256SUMS | sha256sum -c -        # Linux
grep 'mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz$' SHA256SUMS | shasum -a 256 -c -   # Mac
```

On Windows, in PowerShell, compare with the `mewla-windows-amd64.zip` line of `SHA256SUMS`:

```powershell
(Get-FileHash mewla-windows-amd64.zip -Algorithm SHA256).Hash.ToLower()
```
