# Mewla 0.2.7

Answer Claude's questions in Chat, find Plugins and Skills on one Extensions page, and pair a computer with one command.

## New

- Before updating, remove `-web-origin` from your own startup script, or the daemon won't start. Your origins are already saved as addresses. `mewla web` is replaced by `mewla pair`.
- When Claude asks you to choose, Chat shows the question as a card you can answer. Permission and plan-approval prompts show "Waiting for your choice", with a jump to the Terminal.
- The cat appears while Brain loads.
- "N Workers running" now appears once, on the cat's line. Tap it to see the Work list.
- Extensions: Plugins and Skills are on one page. Mewla plugins come first, then each agent's plugins and Skills. Model Providers moved to Settings.
- Connections now say "Computers" and "Pair a computer". `mewla pair` prints the phone QR code and browser links. `mewla address` manages your addresses.
- One computer is connected at a time, and switching to another one is clean.
- Offline, reconnecting and connection states are each shown once, in one place.
- Resource alerts are quieter: the IO and memory-stall thresholds were raised.
- The app is much smaller. Its fonts went from 70 MB to about 0.7 MB since 0.2.5.

## Fixed

- The side drawer sometimes wouldn't close after you switched back to the app.
- In the Terminal, Chinese text uses the system font and lines up with the columns.
- In the Terminal, Claude's ⏺ line is no longer shifted one cell.
- A Claude Session that only talks about permissions no longer shows as blocked.

## Get it

- **Already installed?** Run `mewla update` on your computer, and install the new APK on Android.
- **New here?** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daoleno/mewla/main/install.sh | sh` picks the right file for you. See [Install](https://daoleno.github.io/mewla/docs/install/).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`. Needs `tmux` (`brew install tmux`).
- **Linux x64:** `mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz`
- **Linux ARM:** `mewla-linux-arm64.tar.gz`
- **Android:** `mewla-android-arm64-v0.2.7.apk`. Android asks you to allow installs from unknown sources.
- **iPhone:** [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE)

All changes: [v0.2.6...v0.2.7](https://github.com/daoleno/mewla/compare/v0.2.6...v0.2.7)
