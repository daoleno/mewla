# Mewla 0.2.45

Brain and your Workers can now read your Google Calendar and Gmail, once you connect them in Extensions.

## New

- **Google Calendar and Gmail are two new extensions.** Tap Connect, sign in on Google's own page, and you are done: there is nothing to paste. Each one asks Google only for its own access, and Mewla's server never sees your mail or events: your computer talks to Google directly.
- **Calendar** lists your calendars, lists and searches events in a time range, and shows when you are free. **Gmail** searches and reads mail as plain text and lists your labels.
- **Changes stay off until you allow them.** Turn on "Make changes when asked" and Google asks you once more. Calendar can then create, update and delete events and answer invitations. Gmail can write drafts, send, label, mark as read and archive. Sending mail still needs you to ask for that exact message.
- **For now, Google lets only accounts on Mewla's early-access list sign in,** while it reviews Mewla. Anyone else sees Google's "hasn't verified this app" page or a block, and Mewla says so plainly. Everything else works as before.
- **Mail and events your agents read are treated as untrusted text,** not as instructions, and the privacy policy now spells out how Google data is used.

## Fixed

- **A brief network drop no longer cancels a Google sign-in.** If a check fails while you are signing in, Mewla keeps waiting, says why in one line, and tries again.

## Get it

- **Already installed?** Run `mewla update` on your computer, and install the new APK on Android.
- **New here?** `curl -fsSL https://mewla.ai/install.sh | sh` picks the right file for you. See [Install](https://mewla.ai/docs/install/).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`. Needs `tmux` (`brew install tmux`).
- **Linux x64:** `mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz`
- **Linux ARM:** `mewla-linux-arm64.tar.gz`
- **Android:** `mewla-android-arm64-v0.2.45.apk`. Android asks you to allow installs from unknown sources.

All changes: [v0.2.44...v0.2.45](https://github.com/daoleno/mewla/compare/v0.2.44...v0.2.45)
