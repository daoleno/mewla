# Mewla 0.2.4

Pets stand at full size again, Nori is easier to see, and iPhone has a new TestFlight link.

## New

- Nori is redrawn with a charcoal outline and a cream muzzle, so it stands out from the red seal.
- When Work needs you, the cat waits on its feet on that Work instead of peeking over the seal. Tap it to open the Work.
- iPhone builds now ship as Mewla Preview with a new TestFlight link. The old Zen Preview gets no more updates, so switch to the new link.

## Fixed

- Standing pets are full size again. In 0.2.3 they were drawn at about half size.
- Pets hop out of and back into the seal from the center, without being cut off.

## Get it

- **Already installed?** Run `mewla update` on your computer, and install the new APK on Android.
- **New here?** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/daoleno/mewla/main/install.sh | sh` picks the right file for you. See [Install](https://daoleno.github.io/mewla/docs/install/).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`. Needs `tmux` (`brew install tmux`).
- **Linux x64:** `mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz`
- **Linux ARM:** `mewla-linux-arm64.tar.gz`
- **Android:** `mewla-android-arm64-v0.2.4.apk`. Android asks you to allow installs from unknown sources.
- **iPhone:** [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE)

All changes: [v0.2.3...v0.2.4](https://github.com/daoleno/mewla/compare/v0.2.3...v0.2.4)
