Agents give way to you at the Mac, and handovers to your other computers show up in the conversation. Update the app to 0.2.19 as well; on iPhone it is build 57 in [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE).

## New

- **Handovers show as cards.** When Brain hands a task to another computer, Brain's conversation shows a card such as *Handed to Mac* with *Delivered*, and the answer as *Reply from Mac*. Tap a card to read all of it; **Open on Mac** switches the app to that computer.
- **Stop working together** from the app: in **Settings → Working together**, tap a computer and choose **Stop working together**. Both computers forget each other.
- **An agent's Chrome tabs close when it is done**, so a computer that runs all day doesn't pile up tabs. A tab you dragged into its group closes too; drag it out first to keep it.
- **Agents give way to you at the Mac.** Someone using the Mac's own keyboard or trackpad pauses the agent at once; it carries on 10 seconds after they stop. Agents stay off a locked Mac and can use it again once it's unlocked. Agents can also read a Mac app's window as its buttons, fields and text, to know what to click.

## Fixed

- Commands that need Mewla now say plainly when it isn't running, and how to start it.
