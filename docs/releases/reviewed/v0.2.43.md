Switching computer is one tap from the menu. Update the app to 0.2.43 as well.

## New

- **Tap the computer at the bottom of the menu to switch.** It lists every computer you have paired, marks the one in use and says which are offline, then takes you to the one you pick. With a single computer, the tap opens Settings → Computers.
- **The computer row says what you can act on.** It shows the computer's name, and only when something is wrong (offline, or a problem to fix) a colored word beside it, the same states that put a dot on the menu button.

## Fixed

- **Switching computer is no longer undone by an old notification link.** After opening Brain from a notification, picking another computer used to switch back to the notification's computer.
