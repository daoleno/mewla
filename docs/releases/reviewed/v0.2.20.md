Agents work with a Mac app's own buttons and fields, often in the background, and keep going in other apps while you use the Mac. Update the app to 0.2.20 as well; on iPhone it is build 58 in [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE).

## New

- **Agents work with a Mac app's own controls.** They list the Mac's apps and read any one of them, even one behind others, as numbered buttons, fields and text, then press, fill in or open those by number in the background, without moving your pointer or bringing the app forward (a few actions bring it forward themselves).
  - They can type keys and text into one app alone.
  - They can see one app's window.
  - They can run a short list of steps in one go, such as find, press, type and check.
  - They can click an app's window behind others where macOS allows; elsewhere, and for Chromium-based apps, the app comes to the front for the click.
- **Agents keep working while you use the Mac.** Work in an app behind the one you are using goes on; anything that would share your keyboard and pointer, or touch the app in front, waits until 10 seconds after you stop. **Take over**, **Stop**, the switch and a locked screen still stop all of it.
- **Codex Workers on a Mac can use Codex's own computer use** when your Codex has it set up; `mewla agent-use status` says so. It is Codex's tool, so Watch, Take over, the pause for someone at the Mac and Mewla's log don't cover it; turning off **Agents can use this computer** turns it off for Codex Workers started afterwards.

## Fixed

- The browser tools get ready on their own once Chrome is ready, without anyone asking. `mewla agent-use status` shows what Mewla itself sees, and, when both run 0.2.20, your other computers learn of the change within about 30 seconds of it.
- The display stays on while an agent works, also on battery, and a display that sleeps anyway no longer leaves the agent blind: it is told, and its next action wakes the display.
- Adding a second model connection no longer fails now and then with a "revision conflict".
