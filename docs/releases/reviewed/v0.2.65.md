The app now records why a connection to your computer ended, and no longer mistakes a phone that was asleep for a dead link.

## Fixed

- **A phone that was away no longer makes a healthy connection look broken.** When the app comes back after being suspended, it no longer decides the link has gone silent just because its own timer ran late; frames that arrived in the meantime are read first.
- **Dropped connections now say why.** The app reports the system's error, the close reason and the last message it received, and your computer's log names which side hung up and why a request was refused, so a recurring drop can be traced instead of guessed at.
