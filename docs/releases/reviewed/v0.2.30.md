Press and hold now works the way it does in ChatGPT: a small menu next to your message. Update the app to 0.2.30 as well.

## New

- **A menu that follows your message.** Press and hold one of your messages and a small menu opens beside it with the time sent, **Copy**, **Select Text** and **Edit**. It opens above or below depending on the room, keeps clear of the keyboard, and closes when you tap elsewhere, scroll or press Back.
- **Copy and Retry under every reply.** The row used to appear only under the newest reply. Retry on an older reply asks before it removes the messages after it. A **...** button adds **Select Text**.

## Fixed

- **Selecting reply text works again.** Since 0.2.28, pressing and holding a word in a reply, a link or a code block did not start the system's text selection. It does now, as before.
- **The bottom sheet is gone.** The sheet that covered half the chat is replaced by the menu above.
