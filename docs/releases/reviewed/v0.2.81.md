Phone use gets a floating live view and a much shorter setup, AWS signs in from the app, and Settings is tidier.

## New

- **The agent's screen floats over every page.** While your agent uses your phone (Android, Labs), its live view stays on top of Mewla. Drag it, resize it, or double-tap to enlarge it; it remembers its size.
- **Phone use sets itself up in a few taps.** Mewla walks Settings for you and offers one next step at a time. It restores itself after a reboot or a Wi-Fi change, reconnects in the background, and recovers a black agent display on its own. Agent actions wait up to 30 seconds for a dropped phone to come back. Phone is now in the left menu, under Screen.
- **Phone use recovers after an update.** Installing a new version over a paired phone keeps the running helper working and takes at most one tap on Start. If the phone isn't ready, the agent is told to open Mewla → Phone and why.
- **Sign in to AWS from the app.** The AWS extension now signs in through your browser, and you can paste the address back from another device. The AWS CLI and `~/.aws` are no longer needed. Existing AWS cards show "Sign in again" once.
- **Friends posts show photos and video** in the feed and on the post page.
- **Settings is simpler.** Labs sits above Calendar with one row and one page per feature, Appearance and Pet are plain rows, and the update row is compact, with an Update button.
