Agents on your other computers no longer leave an app in front of yours after choosing its menu item, and they say plainly when an app's windows are on another desktop. Update the app to 0.2.24 as well; on iPhone it is build 62 in [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE).

## Fixed

- **Your other computers.**
  - When an agent chooses an app's menu item, such as ⌘N in Notes, and the app jumps to the front for it, the app you were using is put straight back in front, so your typing still goes to it.
  - When none of an app's windows is on the screen, because they're on another desktop or the app is hidden, agents are told so plainly instead of being told the app can't be read.
  - When a menu item is turned off while its app is in the back, agents are told to bring the app forward when you aren't using the computer, then try again.
