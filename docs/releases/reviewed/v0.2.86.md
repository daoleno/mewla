Mewla now fits foldable iPhones, and Computer use on the Mac is faster.

## New

- **Foldable iPhone support.** The sidebar docks beside the page when the window is wide and tall enough, content stays clear of the side insets, and folding or unfolding sends the terminal one new size instead of several.
- **Computer use is faster.** Mewla reads an app's controls in one request, so Finder takes 55 ms instead of 143 ms. Actions answer with what changed, and Mewla waits until the app goes quiet instead of for a fixed delay.
- **Password fields stay private.** Computer use recognises a Mac's native password fields by role and never reads or shows what is in them.
- **A newer foundation.** The app moves to Expo SDK 58 and React Native 0.88.
- **Update the app and the daemon together.** Computer use needs both on this version.

## Fixed

- **Setup fits short screens.** With a phone on its side, or a foldable's outer screen, the welcome and pairing steps lay out in two columns, so the button no longer covers the text and nothing is cut off.
