Reach your computer from any network: sign in once and your phone and any browser connect over HTTPS, with nothing to open or install.

## New

- Remote access. Run `mewla` and choose "Sign in, reach it from anywhere" (or run `mewla login`), sign in with GitHub at connect.mewla.ai/device, and your computer gets an address like `https://quiet-otter-3f9a.m.mewla.ai`. Your phone and any browser reach it from any network. The relay only forwards encrypted traffic, and every phone or browser still pairs once.
- `mewla login -name studio` renames the address, and `mewla logout` turns remote access off.
- `mewla version` prints the version.
- Cloudflare connects from Extensions with its official MCP server.
- When a connected service changes its tools, the ones that did not change keep working, and you confirm the rest with one tap.
