A tidier Mewla web UI on desktop, and a handful of fixes in chat, Sessions and Extensions.

## New

- **Desktop web pages line up.** Brain, Sessions and the Settings pages share one header, and the title and buttons line up with the content below them at the same width.
- **Menus open beside their button on desktop.** Menus in the web UI now pop up next to what you clicked instead of in the middle of the screen. Phones still use the sheet from the bottom.
- **Dialogs on desktop are roomier**, with more space around their contents.

## Fixed

- **A long Brain reply no longer ends over a screen of blank space** in the web UI.
- **Tables in chat line up**, and short ids and numbers stay on one line.
- **A Session's terminal fills its panel the first time you open it** in the web UI.
- **Extensions no longer flashes "Can't reach" while it is still connecting.**
- **The Auto theme follows your system's light or dark setting as it changes** in the web UI, without a reload.
