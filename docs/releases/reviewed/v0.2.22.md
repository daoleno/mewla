Settings is simpler: one list of your computers, each by its own name. Update the app to 0.2.22 as well; on iPhone it is build 60 in [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE).

## New

- **Simpler Settings.**
  - One list of your computers, each shown by its own name, such as "daoleno's MacBook Pro", with one word for how it is, such as In use, Online or Offline. Update Mewla on each computer so it can tell the app its name; a computer you renamed keeps your name.
  - Tap a computer for its own page: one switch, **Agents can use this computer**, and only the things you can fix, each with its button, such as **Add extension** when Chrome needs the extension, **Open Settings** when the Mac needs a permission, or **Connect** when it isn't connected. Its name, address and **Remove computer** are there too.
  - Computers paired to your phone now work together on their own; there is no separate Working together section any more. Each one's Brain can now hand the others work, so pair only computers you trust with each other. To keep two apart, remove one from the phone; your other computers then forget it.
- **Faster browser steps.** Agents no longer get their own script echoed back after each step.

## Fixed

- Computers you aren't using no longer show as Offline when they are up.
