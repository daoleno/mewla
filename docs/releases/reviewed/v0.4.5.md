## New

- **A dot tells you an update waits.** When a new version of the app or of Mewla on your computer is ready, a small dot sits on the cat, and the same dot on the Settings row until you install it. The update is at the top of Settings.

## Fixed

- **Opening no longer shows "Mewla hit an error."** When the app recovers from a problem on its own, it quietly reports it to your computer instead of covering the screen.
- **Long Brain chats open without stalling.** Opening a long chat does a fraction of the work it did, and older messages load as you scroll back.
- **A message sent while Brain is busy shows once.** It could appear twice in the chat. Update Mewla on your computer for this one.
- **Switching computers is instant.** The app switches the moment you choose another computer.
- **Text on Android 15 stays on one line.** Short labels and Git diff lines no longer wrap onto a second line after you type.
- **Tapping a Calendar run opens it.** A tap right after opening an item could do nothing.
