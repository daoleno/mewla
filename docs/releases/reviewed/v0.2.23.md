A message that couldn't be sent now stays where you sent it, and agents on your other computers work faster and get in your way less. Update the app to 0.2.23 as well; on iPhone it is build 61 in [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE).

## New

- **Your other computers, faster and steadier.**
  - Agents can choose an app's menu item by its shortcut, such as ⌘N for a new note in Notes, even when the app is in the back, so they don't interrupt what you're doing. They bring an app to the front only when it ignores what they send.
  - Opening an app that's already open is instant.
  - Agents can read a whole text field or note, and they say plainly when an app has quit.
  - When you're using the computer, agents wait out the last few seconds or work in apps in the back instead of retrying. If they need to wait longer, they're told when to try again.
  - An agent writing a new note checks that the note is empty first, so it never writes over a note you already have.

## Fixed

- A message that couldn't be sent now stays where you sent it, marked **Not delivered**, with **Retry** and **Discard**. If your computer can't tell whether the agent got it, it says **Delivery unknown**, with **Send again** and **Discard**. It no longer waits forever or moves under your newer messages. Retry can't send it twice: if it had arrived after all, your computer just confirms it.
