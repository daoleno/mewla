The Screen and Phone pages say less, and turning Friends off no longer deletes anything.

## New

- **Screen shows its state in the corner.** Watching, Keep awake, Lid closed and On battery are small labels on the screen instead of sentences that push it down. Disconnected, Mac is locked and Turn on Screen are centred with just a button.
- **Phone setup and the floating screen say less.** Setup shows a spinner instead of narrating each step, and the floating screen shows a spinner while an app opens.

## Changed

- **Turning Friends off keeps everything.** Your account, posts, sign-in and drafts stay, and turning it on again picks up where you left off. Nothing is posted, drafted or pushed while it is off.
- **Deleting your Friends account is its own row.** "Delete Friends account" sits at the bottom of the Friends settings and asks before it does anything.
- **Update the app and the daemon together.** Friends needs both on this version.
