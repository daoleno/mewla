OpenCode Workers are followed through OpenCode's own service, so what you send, answer and stop reaches OpenCode directly and its turns end the moment it says so.

## New

- **OpenCode Workers report their own turns.** Mewla now follows the session it started OpenCode with, so a turn starts and ends when OpenCode says so, a failure arrives with its reason, and two OpenCode Workers in one folder can no longer be mixed up.
- **Messages and Stop reach OpenCode directly.** After its first message, what you or Brain send is delivered to OpenCode and confirmed by OpenCode, not typed into the Terminal, and Stop stops the running turn without a keystroke.
- **OpenCode permission requests are answered from the phone.** When OpenCode asks to run a command or change a file, the card shows what it wants and offers Allow once, Always allow or Reject. A request that was already answered is refused instead of being typed.
