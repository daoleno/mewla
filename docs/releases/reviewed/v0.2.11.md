Mewla now finds its own updates and stays quiet until there is one.

## New

- Mewla checks for a newer release on its own, at launch and when you come back to it. When one exists, a small dot appears on the menu and on Settings, and Settings → About reads "Update to" the new version. Tap it to download, verify and install in place.

## Fixed

- Settings → About no longer shows "Up to date", "Checking…" or check errors. With nothing to update, it shows Mewla and its version, as before.
