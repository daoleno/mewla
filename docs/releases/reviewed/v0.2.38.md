Phone alerts now reach Android with no setup: install the app, pair, and allow notifications. Update the app to 0.2.38 as well.

## New

- **Push notifications work out of the box on Android.** Mewla's own push service is built in, so you no longer need an Expo or Firebase project of your own. When a Session needs you, fails or finishes, or Brain asks you something, the alert arrives even with the app closed, and tapping it opens the right Session or Brain thread. iOS alerts are not available yet.
- **Mewla asks for notification permission after your computer connects,** not the moment the app opens, and it asks only once. If you decline, turn notifications on in Android's settings for Mewla.

## Fixed

- **A dead phone no longer keeps getting pushes.** When Google reports that the phone cannot receive notifications any more, your computer drops it until the app connects again, and logs a clear reason for any push that could not be delivered.
