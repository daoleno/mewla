Pi Workers are followed through an extension Mewla loads into each Pi it starts, so their turns end the moment Pi says so, and what you send and stop reaches Pi without typing into its terminal.

## New

- **Pi Workers report their own turns.** A turn starts and ends when Pi says so, an aborted turn is told apart from a failed one, and the Pi session Mewla started stays the Worker's conversation.
- **Messages and Stop reach Pi directly.** What you or Brain send to a Pi Worker is handed to Pi and confirmed by Pi, not pasted into the Terminal, and Stop aborts the running turn.
