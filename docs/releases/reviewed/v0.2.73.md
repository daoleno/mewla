Calendar shows what each scheduled action does and how its runs went, Stats Activity is a curve, Brain opens its menu from anywhere, and the Gmail and Google Calendar extensions are gone.

## New

- **Calendar shows each scheduled action as its schedule and its runs.** The detail page says when it runs, then what it does in one line, then lists its runs with their status and result. Tap a finished run to read its full result, or a running one to open its Session. There are no IDs on the page.
- **Work and Sessions started by Calendar link back to it.** They show the action's name and date, and tapping the link opens that run.
- **Stats Activity is a curve you can scrub.** Drag across it on the phone, or hover in the web UI, to read any day; tap to open that day.
- **Swipe right anywhere on Brain to open the menu** on the phone.
- **Swipe between Plugins and Skills** on the phone.
- **Brain's Work list shows queued Work too**, not only Work that is running.
- **Desktop web is steadier.** In the web UI, page titles stay in the same place from page to page, Sessions has one New session button, Calendar's month sits beside the agenda, full-window sheets open as dialogs, and keyboard focus shows a clear blue ring.
- **The Gmail and Google Calendar extensions are removed.** When Mewla on your computer updates to this version, it signs out any connected Gmail or Google Calendar account and deletes it. Mewla's own Calendar is not affected.
