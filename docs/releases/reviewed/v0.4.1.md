A polish release for the new app: New session, sheets and pairing work better on short and wide screens, and Sessions say what they need more accurately.

## New

- **New session keeps Start in reach.** Task and Start stay pinned under the list of agents, and with the keyboard up the field and Start share one row. A wide sheet shows more agents at once.
- **A message you send moves to the top** of its thread, and the reply grows under it.

## Fixed

- **Sheets and dialogs keep clear of the screen's sides,** and wide dialogs keep their buttons on screen.
- **Pairing:** a refused paste now says the paste wasn't allowed. A pairing problem scrolls into view. The camera finder notices when you turn the camera on from Settings.
- **Approvals and finished Sessions.** An agent's approval shows up in the app right away. A Session that reports it's done is no longer listed as needing you, and a failed one offers Open instead of Answer.
- **Long threads** with very long one-line commands open and update faster.
