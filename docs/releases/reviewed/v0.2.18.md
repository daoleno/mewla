Your computers can work together, and agents can use your own Chrome and a Mac's screen. Update the app to 0.2.18 as well; on iPhone it is build 56 in [Mewla Preview on TestFlight](https://testflight.apple.com/join/nMQheDCE).

## New

- **Working together.** Connect your computers in one tap: in the app, open **Settings** and tap another computer under **Working together**. Brain on one computer can then hand a task to another computer's Brain, and the reply comes back. From a terminal, run `mewla computers invite` on one computer and `mewla computers add` with that invite on the other.
- **Agents can use your own Chrome**, with the sites you are logged in to, in a tab group of their own. It is one switch, **Agents can use this computer** in **Settings**, on by default. The first time, Chrome needs one extension; the app opens its page in Chrome for you. `mewla agent-use status` says what is missing.
- **Agents can use a Mac's screen** for apps that have no command or website. You always come first: in **Screen**, **Watch** the agent, **Take over**, or **Stop** it. Turning off **Agents can use this computer** stops it.
