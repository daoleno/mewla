Watch now speaks your language and lets you say how long "later" is. Update the app to 0.2.36 as well.

## New

- **Say how long "later" lasts.** Tell Brain "tomorrow" or 「下周再提」 and the problem stays out of the brief for exactly that long (from minutes to a year). Without a time it still rests for a week, and new signals still bring it back early. From the computer: `mewla watch problem label <id> later --for 1d`.
- **Watch follows the language of your app.** The app now tells your computer its language. Problem titles, the Morning brief item in Calendar and the urgent push all use it, in English or Chinese; other languages get English for the item and the push, and Brain still writes to you in yours. With an older app, Watch falls back to the language of your messages, then English.
