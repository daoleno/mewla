Your Android phone now shows what Mewla is doing without opening the app: a live card for a running task, and home-screen widgets. Claude sessions you start from the app are also followed from their first moment, and the app tells which agent a Worker is from the executor that started it.

## New

- **A live card for a running task.** While a Worker runs, Android 16 shows it in the status bar and pinned at the top of the notifications, with the elapsed time and how far along the goal is. Earlier Android versions, and phones where Live updates is turned off for Mewla, get the same card as a normal ongoing notification. It goes away when nothing is running, and once you swipe it away it stays away until new work starts.
- **Answer Brain from the notification.** When Brain needs you, the card shows its choices, such as Ship or Wait. Tapping one opens Mewla on that question and sends your answer once. If the question has already moved on, nothing is sent.
- **Home-screen widgets.** Two widgets, small and medium, show how many Workers are running and when Brain needs you, with the choices on the medium one. They refresh when something changes, and tapping opens Mewla. This works for computers you reached through Mewla Link too.
- **Widgets cannot do more than look.** A widget reads from your computer with its own read-only key, never your pairing. Revoking the phone from the computer stops its widgets too; for a phone connected through Mewla Link they stop updating and show Open Mewla after 12 hours. Update your computer with `mewla update` for this to work.
- **Claude sessions started from the app are followed from their first moment.** Like Workers Mewla starts itself, they now run with their own conversation id and Mewla's hook file, so a turn's start and end, and a permission prompt, reach Mewla straight from Claude. `mewla worker approvals` and `approve` work on them too. A session that already names a conversation or carries your own `--settings` is left as it is.

## Fixed

- **Updates reach the phone while the app is closed.** Live updates arrive as quiet background messages and no longer risk showing an empty notification.
- **A Worker started from a custom executor shows as the agent it runs.** An executor you named yourself in `executors.toml` (a Codex wrapper, a Claude with fixed flags) used to look like a plain terminal, and a command that only mentioned an agent could be taken for one. The daemon now records which executor started each Worker and sends its agent family with it; the app uses that for the icon, the title and the agent-specific prompts. Update the app and the computer together: a computer on an older version does not send it, and its Workers show as terminals until it updates.
