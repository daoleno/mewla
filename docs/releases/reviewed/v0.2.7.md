Answer Claude's questions right in Chat, without switching to the Terminal.

## New

- When Claude asks you to choose, Chat shows the question as a card. Pick an option, tick several where allowed, or type your own answer in Other, then tap Send answer. Once it's answered, from the card or the Terminal, the card becomes one Answered line.
- Claude's permission and plan-approval prompts now show as "Waiting for your choice", with Claude's own options and a jump to the Terminal.

## Fixed

- A Claude Session that only talks about permissions no longer shows as blocked.
