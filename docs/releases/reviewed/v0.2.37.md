Brain opens in well under a second on a computer with a long history, and its chat no longer empties to "Waking up" while it catches up. Update the app to 0.2.37 as well.

## New

- **Brain opens fast whatever its history.** Brain's chat is read from the end of the host's transcript instead of from its start, so the first messages after a daemon restart arrive in a fraction of a second, even when that transcript has grown to hundreds of MB. What you see is the same conversation as before.

## Fixed

- **No empty "Waking up" over a conversation you were reading.** When Brain's chat is rebuilt, it starts from the messages it last showed and updates them in place when the daemon answers, instead of clearing to the waking cat.
- **No reply buttons without a message.** Quick replies such as "处理" and "稍后" show only under Brain's message they answer, never alone on an empty screen.
