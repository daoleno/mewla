Calendar events, deadlines and reminders now alert you before they are due, and a Worker's question or approval always reaches Brain.

## New

- **Calendar alerts you won't miss.** Events notify 10 minutes early, deadlines 30 minutes early, and reminders on time. Brain or you can set the lead time per item.
- **A full-screen alert card in the app** with Done, Snooze 5, Snooze 15 and Open. It stays up until someone handles it, and handling it on one device clears it on the others.
- **Android alarms that work on a locked or offline phone.** Alerts have their own notification channel, light up the lock screen with a full-screen alert, and fire from an on-device alarm even without a connection. Android 14 and later may ask you to allow full-screen alerts.
- **Strong calendar alerts** is one switch in Settings, on by default. Turn it off for ordinary notifications.

## Fixed

- **A Worker's question or permission prompt always reaches Brain,** including one raised by a background sub-agent and one that comes after the Worker has already reported. Brain answers every blocked Worker the same way.
- **Sending to a busy Worker queues the message** instead of failing.
- **Connections keep the full tool description** instead of cutting it at 1,000 characters. Tools whose descriptions used to be cut show as updated and need your confirmation once.
- **The Extensions view shows long tool descriptions** in full when you tap them.
