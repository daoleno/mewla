Your phone now goes straight to your computer when it can, and says so. Update the app to 0.2.45 as well.

## New

- **A phone paired through Mewla Link uses your Wi-Fi/LAN or Tailscale when it can reach the computer, and the relay otherwise.** Screen and the rest of the app share the choice. The Screen badge says **LAN**, **Tailscale** or **Relay**, and a direct path cuts the round trip from hundreds of milliseconds to a few. The computer must be listening on that network (`mewla --lan`); one that listens on loopback only keeps using the relay.
- **The phone proves it is talking to your computer before it trusts a direct address.** It only tries private-network addresses your computer announced to it, over a connection pinned to the computer's key, and it sends nothing from this phone until the computer has signed a fresh challenge. A wrong or reused address fails and the relay is used.

## Fixed

- **Losing the direct address no longer ends the Screen.** The connection falls back to the relay, and a Screen viewer that was on a direct path retries once on its own. A phone that joins Wi-Fi after connecting through the relay moves onto the direct path within about half a minute.
