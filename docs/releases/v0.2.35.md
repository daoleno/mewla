# Mewla 0.2.35

The morning brief is now an ordinary Calendar item, so you can see when it comes and change it. Update the app to 0.2.35 as well.

## New

- **The morning brief lives in Calendar.** Watch adds one daily item, "Morning brief" (「早报」 if you write to Brain in Chinese), at the next 08:30 in your time zone. Move it, change how it repeats, or cancel it in Calendar or with `mewla calendar update`, and the brief follows. Cancel it and briefs stop for good; Mewla won't add it again. `mewla watch brief` still sends one on demand.
- **No second reminder.** The phone shows no notification of its own for this item. You hear from Brain only when there is something to say.

## Fixed

- **No brief in the middle of the night.** A daemon that was down at the brief time still briefs up to three and a half hours late. After that it skips that day instead.

## Get it

- **Already installed?** Run `mewla update` on your computer, and install the new APK on Android.
- **New here?** `curl -fsSL https://mewla.ai/install.sh | sh` picks the right file for you. See [Install](https://mewla.ai/docs/install/).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`. Needs `tmux` (`brew install tmux`).
- **Linux x64:** `mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz`
- **Linux ARM:** `mewla-linux-arm64.tar.gz`
- **Android:** `mewla-android-arm64-v0.2.35.apk`. Android asks you to allow installs from unknown sources.

All changes: [v0.2.34...v0.2.35](https://github.com/daoleno/mewla/compare/v0.2.34...v0.2.35)
