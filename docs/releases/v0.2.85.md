# Mewla 0.2.85

The Screen and Phone pages say less, and turning Friends off no longer deletes anything.

## New

- **Screen shows its state in the corner.** Watching, Keep awake, Lid closed and On battery are small labels on the screen instead of sentences that push it down. Disconnected, Mac is locked and Turn on Screen are centred with just a button.
- **Phone setup and the floating screen say less.** Setup shows a spinner instead of narrating each step, and the floating screen shows a spinner while an app opens.
- **Delete Friends account is its own row.** It sits at the bottom of the Friends settings and asks before it does anything.
- **Update the app and the daemon together.** Friends needs both on this version.

## Fixed

- **Turning Friends off no longer deletes your account.** Your account, posts, sign-in and drafts stay, and turning it on again picks up where you left off. Nothing is posted, drafted or pushed while it is off.

## Get it

- **Already installed?** Run `mewla update` on your computer, and install the new APK on Android.
- **New here?** `curl -fsSL https://mewla.ai/install.sh | sh` picks the right file for you. See [Install](https://mewla.ai/docs/install/).
- **Mac (Apple Silicon):** `mewla-darwin-arm64.tar.gz`. Needs `tmux` (`brew install tmux`).
- **Linux x64:** `mewla-linux-amd64.tar.gz`
- **Linux ARM:** `mewla-linux-arm64.tar.gz`
- **Android:** `mewla-android-arm64-v0.2.85.apk`. Android asks you to allow installs from unknown sources.

All changes: [v0.2.84...v0.2.85](https://github.com/daoleno/mewla/compare/v0.2.84...v0.2.85)
