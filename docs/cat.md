# The cat's house

Brain's cat has a small house. It is a tab of its own, **Cat**, beside Brain and
Sessions at the top of the App: tap it and you are there. It needs no setup and
has no menus: you tap things in the picture. Swipe to Brain or Sessions, or tap
their tabs, to leave.

The house is painted as a gouache storybook. The cat is one round, striped
silhouette; it only changes pose.

## The loop

Brain works, the cat travels, it writes to you, you look after it at home, and
the bond that grows changes what it does and what it brings back.

| Brain | The cat |
| --- | --- |
| Idle | At home, doing its own things. The light follows the phone's clock (dusk from about 6 pm, night by 8:30 pm, dawn from about 5:30 am) |
| A Worker starts a Work | The bag flies from its hook, the cat hops to the window and slips out, taking the snack you packed |
| The Work runs | A postcard shortly after it leaves, and one more if the Work runs long |
| The Work is accepted | It comes home with a souvenir for the shelf, stars for the jar and often a new hobby |
| Something waits on you | A small sign hangs by the cat, or on its empty hook while it is away. Tap it to go back to Brain |

A Work that is cancelled brings the cat home with nothing.

## The first visit

Nothing explains itself in words. The cat greets you, then shows you one thing
at a time by walking over to it and thinking of it while it glows:

1. a ring around the cat: pet it;
2. the mailbox: its welcome note;
3. the bowl: feed it (it starts with two fish to share);
4. the window: the yard, where today's treats are waiting;
5. the bag: pack a snack for its next trip.

Each step is shown once, and the daemon remembers which ones you have done and
which new things you have already met, so another device does not show them
again. Anything new later introduces itself
the same way: a hobby's prop, the jar's first star, a level.

## What you can tap

- **The cat** answers every tap: a purr, a look, a little hop. When you come
  back after a while it greets you, more warmly after a long gap or as the bond
  grows, and it may ask for the food you gave it last.
- **The bowl** opens the pantry. It eats two meals a day and then shakes its head.
  Food left while it is away waits in the bowl, and it eats it when it is home.
  The food it has been given most becomes its favourite.
- **The yarn** is a game it loves.
- **A hobby's prop** (the toolbox, easel, newspaper, stove, ukulele, watering
  can, letters, telescope) asks it to do that hobby. Once a day gardening brings
  a berry to the pantry and letters bring a letter to the mailbox.
- **The mailbox** shows a new postcard and keeps it in the **album**; with none
  waiting it opens the album.
- **The shelf** holds the souvenirs. Tap one to see the Work it came from and the
  hobby that trip taught.
- **The window** is the yard. A few treats grow each day: pick them up, and tap
  one in the bag to pack it before the cat leaves. Once it fishes, it sits by
  the pond by day (tap it: once a day it catches a fish); once it camps, its tent
  stands there at night.
- **The jar** shows the stars and the shape of what the next level brings.
- **The picture frames** on the wall show its latest postcards.
- **A visiting bird** now and then lands on the sill and leaves a berry or a fish
  for the pantry.

## The bond

Care drops stars into the jar, a few a day for each kind so it cannot be ground:

| Care | Stars |
| --- | --- |
| Pet it | 1, three times a day |
| Feed it | 2, twice a day; one more for its favourite |
| Play with the yarn | 2, twice a day |
| Ask for a hobby | 1, three times a day |
| Read a postcard | 1, four times a day |
| Keep a visitor's gift | 1, twice a day |
| An accepted Work | 3 when it comes home, 2 more if you packed it a snack |

There are ten levels (at 0, 6, 16, 30, 48, 70, 96, 126, 160 and 200 stars). A
level lets the cat hold one more hobby, hangs one more picture frame (from level
2), grows one more treat in the yard (at levels 3 and 6), and from level 4 it
meets friends on its trips and now and then finds a rare place. Days you miss
cost nothing: nothing decays, and a new day simply allows new stars.

### Hobbies

Each place teaches a hobby: the workshop mending, the harbour fishing, the studio
painting, the library the newspaper, the garden gardening, the observatory
stargazing, the market and the kitchen cooking, the post office and the night
desk letters, the station music, and the trail camping. An accepted Work teaches
its place's hobby if the bond has room for one more; otherwise the cat keeps it
in mind and takes it up at the next level. A new hobby brings its prop into the
room (fishing and camping happen in the yard), and once the cat knows it, it
also does it on its own and on its postcards.

### Memory and mood

The daemon keeps the cat's last 60 moments (petted, fed what, played, which
hobby, learnt, levelled, left, came back, a gift) and a mood: away, excited
(just home), missed (your first visit after most of a day), full, happy (cared
for lately) or calm. The page uses them for its greeting and its requests. They
are where the cat's words will come from once it can talk; there is no chat yet.

## Postcards

A postcard is composed, not painted: an empty picture of a place, the hour as a
wash of light, the day's weather, the cat doing something there, a friend it
met, and the snack it took. The daemon picks each piece and keeps the choice,
so a card looks the same every time it is opened, on every device.

- **The place** comes from the Work's title by fixed keywords (studio, harbour,
  library, workshop, garden, observatory, market, kitchen, post office, station,
  night desk, and a trail for anything else), so it works offline and cannot
  leak: only the place leaves the title, never its words. A cat at level 4 or
  more sometimes writes a long trip's card from a rare place instead: the
  lighthouse, a snowy hot spring, the flower hills, a moonlit lake.
- **What it does** is one of the place's own things. It favours the hobby the
  place teaches, then hobbies it knows; it never shows another hobby it has not
  met.
- **The hour** is when the card was sent (dawn, day, dusk, night); the
  observatory, the night desk and the lake are only ever at night and the
  lighthouse at dusk. **The weather** is one per day; snow falls only from
  December to February, and indoors it is only in the words.
- **Friends** (a frog, a snail, a rabbit, a hedgehog, a bird by day, an owl at
  night) join from level 4, more often as the bond grows.

No two cards in the album show the same scene (place, doing, hour, weather and
friend): a crowded album varies the weather and brings friends to keep them
apart. The line is written from the same pieces in the phone's language, chosen
the way the rest of the App does: the device locale, Chinese when it starts with
`zh` and English otherwise. Besides trip cards, the cat writes a welcome note on
the first visit and, once it has taken up letters, a letter from home.

At most one postcard comes when a Work starts and one when it runs long (45
minutes), and at least 10 minutes pass between any two, so many Works starting
at once never flood the album. A Work that is already running when the daemon
first learns of the cat sends nothing: history is not backfilled.

The souvenir keeps the title of your own Work, on your own shelf only.

## The living room

- **At home** it reads, writes, has tea, eats, plays with yarn, naps on its bed,
  and does the hobbies it knows; at night it sleeps on its cushion or watches the
  stars. What it picks follows the hour, chance and what you ask of it most.
- **Small life**: clouds, a butterfly, a lamp that flickers at night with
  crickets, and the visiting bird.
- **Sound** is made in the page, with no audio files: a quiet room tone, paper,
  a purr, a clink for each star, small sounds for each hobby. It starts with the
  first tap and stops whenever the tab is not on screen. There is no switch.

## Where it lives

The daemon keeps the state per computer (`catpark/state.json` in the state
directory) and derives it from Brain's snapshots and the Work record. Every
postcard and souvenir is keyed by Work and written to disk before it is
announced, so a restart repeats nothing. App and web UI show the same state
because both read it from the daemon over the connection they already have:
`cat_get` and `cat_state`, and `cat_act` with one of a fixed list of acts
(`visit`, `pet`, `feed`, `play`, `hobby`, `collect`, `pack`, `seen`, `gift`,
`learn`); the daemon checks every act and its argument and ignores the rest.
A state written by an older version of the game is kept beside it
(`state.json.v1`) and a new cat starts.

The game is one web page the daemon carries (`/cat/`). The phone cannot open a
daemon URL in a WebView (its connection is a pinned tunnel on a port that
changes per launch), so the app downloads the page with signed requests into a
cache keyed by its version, checks each file against the manifest's digest, and
opens it from disk. The page holds no credentials and has no network access; it
can ask the app only for the acts above. After the first visit it opens offline
with the last state it knew. The web UI frames the same page from `/cat/`: the
web UI's content security policy allows `frame-src 'self'`, and only `/cat/`
allows being framed, by the web UI itself.

The cat's world is one file, `daemon/catpark/web/catalog.js`: the levels, foods,
hobbies, places with where the cat stands in each picture, rare places, friends,
weathers, and every word in both languages. The page draws from it and the
daemon reads the same file to compose postcards. A new language is a new set of
words there; tests fail if any place, act, friend, food or hobby lacks a picture
or a word in either language. The pictures are generated art in the page's `a/`
folder.

The handwritten line uses a subset of LXGW WenKai (SIL OFL 1.1); its notice is in
the repository's third-party assets list.
