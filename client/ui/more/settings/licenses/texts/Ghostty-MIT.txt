Ghostty VT (libghostty-vt) — third-party notice
================================================

Mewla's native terminal support uses libghostty-vt, built from
upstream Ghostty. The following MIT license text applies to that library
and must be preserved when redistributing apps or prebuilt native artifacts.

Source: https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty
Release: libghostty-vt 1.0.1 (tag vt-1.0.1), pinned in native/terminal-vt/native.lock.json

libghostty-vt also compiles in the following third-party code. Their license
texts are in the app, under Settings > Open-source licenses:

  Highway 1.2.0           Apache-2.0 OR BSD-3-Clause
  simdutf 9.0.0           Apache-2.0 OR MIT
  uucode 0.2.0            MIT AND Unicode-3.0 (the Unicode data tables)
  Wuffs 0.4.0-alpha.10    Apache-2.0 OR MIT
  translate-c 2.0.0       MIT

Mewla build entrypoint: scripts/build-libghostty.sh
Packaging: client/android/app/build.gradle.kts copies this file into
the APK at assets/notices/GHOSTTY-MIT.txt; client/ios/project.yml copies
it to GHOSTTY-MIT.txt at the root of the iOS app bundle.
