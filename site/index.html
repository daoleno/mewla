One sign-in. Any network.

Install it on your computer, sign in once, and scan with your phone. No port to open, no domain, no VPN.

    Install

    curl -fsSL https://mewla.ai/install.sh | sh
    irm https://mewla.ai/install.ps1 | iex

    Checksum-verified. No admin rights, no telemetry.
