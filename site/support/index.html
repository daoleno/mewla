Support

Stuck, or found a bug? Most answers are in the docs. If not, ask us. 需要帮助？中文提问也可以。

Common questions

  • How do I get started? Install mewla on your computer, start it, and pair the app.
  • The phone cannot connect to my computer.
  • How do I reach my computer away from home? Remote access with mewla login, or your own Tailscale or tunnel.
  • It paired, but no Sessions appear, or agents fail to start.
  • I get no notifications.
  • How do I see my Mac’s screen?
  • Which agents and models work? And what can they do on my computer?
  • How do I update?
  • How do I delete my account? Run mewla logout -delete-account on a signed-in computer.
  • Known issues in the current release.

Running mewla doctor on your computer checks the most common problems and tells you what to fix.

Ask for help

  • GitHub: open an issue for bugs, questions and ideas. Please include the output of mewla version and mewla doctor, your phone and OS version, and the steps that show the problem. Remove addresses and pairing links before you post.
  • Email: support@mewla.ai, for anything you would rather not post in public, including account and privacy requests.
  • Security issues: report them privately, as described in the security policy. Please do not open a public issue.

We read every message and usually answer within a few days.
