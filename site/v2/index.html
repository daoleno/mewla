Landing · three directions

Pick the one that feels like Zen.

Same product, same words, three different moods. All three open with the sleeping cat as the mark, cover Brain first and every other feature once, and end on the same install steps. Only the art direction changes.

A · Nap

Asleep in a warm meadow.

Warm cream and a soft serif. A sky with drifting clouds, two hills, and the cat asleep on the grass with z's floating up. A second cat walks past as you scroll.

  • The cat naps, then stirs when you stop scrolling
  • Sticky phone panel walks beside the five Brain steps
  • Warmest of the three; reads most like a storybook
Open direction A

B · A day with Brain

The cat acts out your day.

Cool mint, a room with a window and a lamp. Scrolling runs a clock forward and swaps the cat's pose to match each beat: asleep, stretching, sitting, walking, kneading.

  • Five poses, one per Brain beat, with a caption
  • Clock hand tracks how far through the day you are
  • Most motion; the mascot carries the whole page
Open direction B

C · Curled

A real 3D cat you can poke.

Periwinkle and geometric type. The hero cat is a low-poly model built in code with three.js — drag to look around it, click to wake it. It breathes while it sleeps.

  • Drag orbits the camera; click opens one eye
  • Flat-shaded facets, real contact shadow
  • The 2D poses still cameo through the rest of the page
Open direction C

Every cat here is drawn by hand in SVG or assembled from primitives in three.js — nothing traced or reused. Screenshots are the real app on demo data. Once you pick a direction, the other two come out and that one moves to the site root.
